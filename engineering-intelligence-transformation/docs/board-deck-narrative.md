# 12-Slide Board Narrative

1. Engineering Intelligence Transformation — from reactive engineering to AI-amplified supervised autonomous systems.
2. The Scaling Tax — knowledge fragmentation, slow onboarding, repeat incidents, architecture drift and rising MTTR.
3. Strategic Opportunity — embed private intelligence into IDE, PR, CI/CD, incidents and governance.
4. What This Is Not — not a chatbot, not public AI, not engineer replacement.
5. Target Architecture — AI Gateway, governed retrieval, enterprise LLM, observability, policy and runbooks.
6. Phase 1: Knowledge Layer — citation-backed answers over repos, ADRs, runbooks and tickets.
7. Phase 2: PR Intelligence — PR Guardian catches regression, IaC, security and architecture risks.
8. Phase 3: Incident Intelligence — correlate failed deployments with logs, recent changes and historical fixes.
9. Phase 4-5: Predictive + Self-Healing — risk scoring, drift detection and allow-listed remediation.
10. Financial Impact — capacity recovery and incident savings.
11. Governance — RBAC, audit, private networking, prompt-injection controls and human gates.
12. Strategic Outcome — AI-amplified engineering, semi-autonomous infrastructure and measurable risk.
