# Executive Memo

## Subject
Private Engineering Intelligence Platform for velocity, resilience and risk control.

## Executive Summary
Engineering is paying a scaling tax: knowledge fragmentation, slow onboarding, growing regression risk, repeat incidents, high MTTR and architecture drift. Adding headcount alone will not solve it. We should build a private Engineering Intelligence Platform that turns repos, work items, ADRs, CI/CD history, logs and runbooks into a governed SDLC intelligence layer.

## Decision Requested
Approve a 24-month phased program with a 90-day foundation milestone.

## What This Is
- Secure internal cognitive infrastructure.
- Governed RAG with citations.
- AI agents embedded into PRs, deployments and incidents.
- Policy-aware automation with human gates.

## What This Is Not
- Not public chatbot usage.
- Not engineer replacement.
- Not unrestricted autonomous production mutation.

## Executive Guardrails
- Private tenant/network only.
- RBAC before retrieval.
- No secrets embedded.
- Audit all prompts, retrieved sources and actions.
- Human approval for high-risk production actions.
- FinOps dashboard from day one.
